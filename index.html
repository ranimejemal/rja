(Product vision)

Build once, sell repeatedly

We connect people who build automations with businesses that need them, making buying and configuring an automation as straightforward as buying a digital product.

Ranime Jemal Agency is an AI automation marketplace where creators sell reusable n8n workflows and businesses personalize them for the way they work.

Founded by Ranime Jemal, born from the belief that no business should need to understand n8n, edit JSON, or build workflows from scratch. automation made simple.

The marketplace works for both sides, hand in hand:

1.

For sellers: automation creators publish an n8n workflow once, define exactly what buyers can customize, and sell it to many businesses, with optional setup and custom services.

2.

For buyers: businesses find an automation for a specific problem, personalize it with clear settings, connect their tools, and activate it with step-by-step guidance.

Behind both sides, the platform handles validation, licensing, secure credential connections and support. Every workflow is reviewed before it is published, credentials stay encrypted and separate from templates, and updates never silently break an installation.

Ready-made automations across seven business categories (marketing, sales, customer support, e-commerce, productivity, finance and IT operations), each available as a download, guided setup, hosted automation or custom implementation.
