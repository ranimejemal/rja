(How it works — buyers)

Buying an automation, made simple.

Find an automation for a specific business problem, personalize it, connect your tools, and get it working with clear instructions.

  1. Stage 01

    Discover an automation

    As a business owner, search for a solution to a repetitive task and find an automation without needing to understand the underlying technology.

    • Search by product names, categories, use cases, and supported integrations.
    • Filter by price, delivery type, supported apps, difficulty, and customer ratings.
    • Every listing states what the automation does and what it does not do.
    • Products display supported n8n versions or relevant compatibility information.
  2. Stage 02

    Inspect a product

    Understand the product's capabilities, requirements, and limitations before paying.

    • Business problem solved, seller profile, and a live or recorded demonstration.
    • Example input and output, and a workflow overview.
    • Supported integrations, required accounts and subscriptions, minimum technical requirements.
    • Customization options, license and usage rights, installation difficulty.
    • Price and recurring fees, update and support policy, reviews from verified buyers.
  3. Stage 03

    Choose a purchase option

    Select the delivery option that fits your technical skills and budget.

    • Download — receive a workflow template and setup guide.
    • Guided setup — the template plus configuration assistance.
    • Hosted automation — run the workflow on the platform, if the seller offers a hosted version.
    • Custom implementation — request additional development from the seller.
    One-time and recurring prices are clearly distinguished. Required third-party accounts and possible usage charges are shown. License and refund conditions are accessible before payment.
  4. Stage 04

    Checkout & payment

    Checkout asks for the minimum necessary information. Guests provide an email address and get an account-creation or secure access flow; registered users confirm their billing details.

    • Review the order.
    • Confirm the selected product and license.
    • Accept the applicable terms.
    • Pay using an available payment method.
    • Payment is verified through the payment provider's server-side confirmation.
    • The order is created and the purchased entitlement granted.
    • A receipt and access instructions are sent.
    Payment declinedPayment pendingDuplicate payment notificationPaid — delivery temporarily unavailableRefund or chargeback initiated
    Paid access is never granted solely because a browser redirects to a success page.
  5. Stage 05

    My Automations

    Manage all your purchased automations in one place. Open the product, continue configuration, download the template, access documentation, or request support.

    PurchasedSetup requiredIntegrations requiredReady to testActivePausedError detectedUpdate available
  6. Stage 06

    Customize the automation

    Personalize a purchased automation using clear settings so it behaves appropriately for your business — without editing workflow code.

(Configuration wizard)

Seven steps to active.

Step 01

Business profile

  • Business name
  • Industry
  • Preferred language
  • Time zone
  • Working hours
  • Brand voice
  • Relevant business rules
Step 02

Select capabilities

Enable only the supported capabilities included in your license.

  • Categorize enquiries
  • Summarize customer messages
  • Draft responses
  • Create CRM records
  • Notify team members
Step 03

Connect integrations

Connect an email provider, CRM, form service, or spreadsheet. Secure OAuth where possible, otherwise a secure credential-entry flow. Credentials are encrypted, access-controlled, and excluded from exported workflow files and ordinary logs.

Step 04

Business rules

  • Which enquiries count as high priority
  • Which information must be collected
  • Which messages require human approval
  • Which destination receives notifications
  • What to do when required information is missing
Step 05

Preview

The system summarizes your selected settings and highlights missing requirements.

Step 06

Test

Send a sample input and see the output, any errors, and whether the workflow passed its checks.

Step 07

Activate

Enabled only when required integrations and configuration checks pass. Consequential actions — sending messages, modifying business records — get authorization and approval controls.
