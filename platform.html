(Essential product rules)

Twelve rules we don't bend.

  1. 01

    Explicit license

    Every purchase grants an explicit license and entitlement.

  2. 02

    Defined delivery

    Every published product has a defined delivery method.

  3. 03

    Mapped settings

    Every buyer-editable setting maps to a validated workflow parameter.

  4. 04

    Separate credentials

    Credentials are managed separately from workflow templates.

  5. 05

    Tenant isolation

    Customer configurations and execution data are isolated between tenants.

  6. 06

    Untrusted uploads

    Workflow uploads are untrusted until validated and reviewed.

  7. 07

    Safe updates

    Product updates do not silently break existing customer installations.

  8. 08

    Visible compatibility

    Buyers can see compatibility requirements before purchasing.

  9. 09

    Private secrets

    Sellers cannot access customer secrets by default.

  10. 10

    Recorded events

    The platform records important administrative, payment, configuration, and activation events.

  11. 11

    Defined limits

    Workflows have defined failure handling, execution limits, and support boundaries.

  12. 12

    Explicit policies

    Refunds, seller commissions, payouts, and subscription renewals have explicit policies.

(Launch plan)

Six phases to a full marketplace.

We start with downloadable n8n templates and guided customization, and prove that customers purchase and successfully configure the products before taking responsibility for running every customer's automation on our own infrastructure.

Phase 01

Marketplace foundation

Authentication, product listings, search, seller profiles, checkout, order records, and buyer/seller dashboards.

Phase 02

Digital product delivery

Validated workflow templates and documentation, delivered through authorized, expiring download links.

Phase 03

Customization system

Seller-defined configuration schemas, parameter mapping, input validation, and a buyer configuration wizard.

Phase 04

Secure integration setup

Credential connections, OAuth where supported, required integration checks, and test workflows.

Phase 05

Hosted automation

Isolated customer instances or controlled deployments, activation controls, execution monitoring, usage limits, billing, and failure handling.

Phase 06

Marketplace growth

Ratings, version updates, subscriptions, seller commissions, analytics, and optional professional customization services.
