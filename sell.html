(Sell an automation)

Build once, sell repeatedly.

Earn from your technical expertise, and offer optional installation and customization services.

  1. Stage 01

    Become a seller

    Establish a seller profile so customers can discover and trust your products.

    • Display name and profile
    • Business or creator details
    • Short biography
    • Technical specialties
    • Contact and support preferences
    • Required payout and tax information, as applicable
    Seller verification and payout eligibility depend on the platform's payment provider and supported countries.
  2. Stage 02

    Create a product

    • Product name, category, and business use case
    • Description, benefits, and limitations
    • Screenshots or demonstration video
    • Price and license
    • Required applications
    • n8n version and technical requirements
    • Setup and support policy
    • Version number and changelog

    Upload an n8n workflow JSON file, or create a product through an authorized integration with your development environment. The platform parses the file and checks its structure.

    Uploading a workflow does not mean it is safe or ready to publish. It must pass security checks and any required manual review before it can be distributed or executed.
  3. Stage 03

    Define customization

    Open the Customization Builder and choose which settings buyers may change. See below ↓

  4. Stage 04

    Test the product

    Complete the pre-publish checklist. See below ↓

  5. Stage 05

    Publish the listing

    Submit the product for review. Published products become searchable and purchasable.

(Customization Builder)

The platform's key feature.

For each field, define a label, description, default value, validation rules, allowed choices, and whether it is required — then map it to a specific parameter in the automation.

SettingTypeBuyer can edit?
Business nameTextYes
Response languageDropdownYes
Brand voiceDropdownYes
CRM integrationSupported choicesYes
Lead qualification thresholdNumber or predefined rangeYes
Internal API credentialsSecretOnly through secure connection flow
Core workflow structureManaged by sellerNo, unless explicitly exposed
Security-critical settingsRestrictedNo ordinary buyer editing
A field that is visible in the customization form but not connected to the workflow is not a functional customization.
