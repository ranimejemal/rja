Social Media Content Scheduler

About

Turns a content calendar into ready-to-post captions and publishes them on schedule across your social accounts. Posting consistently across platforms takes hours every week. Workflow: Calendar → Write captions → Approve → Publish. Supported integrations: Google Sheets, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn. Difficulty: Beginner. Sold by Ranime Jemal Agency.

Example input: A calendar row: topic, platform, date, image link. Example output: An approved caption with hashtags, published at the scheduled time. Required accounts: Google account, Meta Business account, LinkedIn page, OpenAI API key. Technical requirements: n8n 1.x (self-hosted or n8n Cloud), not needed with the hosted option. You can customize: Brand voice, Posting language, Hashtag rules, Approval required (yes/no).
